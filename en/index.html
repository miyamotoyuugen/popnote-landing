The moment you open an app, take a quick look at a note you wrote to yourself.

PopNote pops up a sticky note you wrote yourself whenever you open an app you've picked. It doesn't remind you at a set time or place. It reminds you right when you're about to do the thing.

Ways to use it

  1. Before you shop, check your list

    Open a shopping app and your shopping list and this month's budget pop up first. Buy what you came for.

  2. Before you trade, check your rules

    Open your brokerage or crypto app and see the investing rules you set for yourself, like "Don't chase highs" or "No more than 10% of my funds on a single trade."

  3. Before you scroll, check your goal

    Open a social app and see a line you wrote to yourself, a reminder of what you want to focus on today.

FAQ

Will PopNote block me from using my apps?

No. It just reminds you. Once you've read the note, you can use the app as usual.

How does the note show up?

Your choice: as a notification banner or full screen.

Can I add more than one note to the same app?

Yes. Each app can have multiple notes.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad. You set it up with Shortcuts, the app that comes built into iOS and iPadOS.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your notes stay on your device, and no usage data is uploaded.
